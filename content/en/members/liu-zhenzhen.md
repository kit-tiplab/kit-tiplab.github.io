## About Me

I am a Researcher at the Faculty of Engineering, Kitami Institute of Technology, and a member of the Text Information Processing Laboratory (TiP Lab). I received my Ph.D. in Engineering from Kitami Institute of Technology in 2023.

My research focuses on **tourism informatics** and **natural language processing (NLP)**. In particular, I investigate methods to extract and analyze tourist motivations, destination focus points, and sentiment from multilingual online reviews using transformer models, machine learning, and statistical analysis. Additionally, I work on automated fake and spam review detection for e-commerce platforms and cross-lingual translation analysis. Prior to my doctoral studies, I also gained industry experience as a System Engineer in Tokyo and Hangzhou, developing software systems and AI conversational chatbots.

## Research Interests

Tourism Informatics · Natural Language Processing · Sentiment & Review Analysis · Tourist Motivation & Focus Point Extraction · Spam & Fake Review Detection · Cross-Lingual NLP & Sarcasm Detection
