## About Me

I am an Associate Professor at the Faculty of Engineering, Kitami Institute of Technology, and a member of the Text Information Processing Laboratory (TiP Lab). My research centers on natural language processing (NLP) and applied machine learning, focusing primarily on areas where I lead investigation:

- **Cross-Lingual Transfer Learning & Linguistic Similarity**: Developing methods to predict and select optimal transfer languages based on linguistic and typological similarity, enabling effective zero-shot cross-lingual transfer learning for low-resource languages and neural machine translation.
- **Cyberbullying & Abusive Language Detection**: Designing efficient machine learning models and linguistically grounded representations to identify online toxicity, cyberbullying, and abusive language, including feature density estimation to optimize classifier training.
- **Generative AI & LLMs in Education**: Investigating the integration of large language models into higher education—specifically personalized English language education in Japan—fostering tailored learning, student engagement, and critical thinking skills.
- **Tourism Informatics**: Applying transformer models and natural language processing to extract and evaluate inbound tourist motivations and travel behaviors from multilingual online reviews.

Prior to my academic career in Japan, I worked in industry as a Data Scientist at Alma Mediapartners in Finland, combining hands-on machine learning engineering with academic research.
