## About Me

I build bias-aware, edge-ready speech and language models for under-represented languages. Currently a Ph.D. candidate at the Kitami Institute of Technology , I am developing unified Multimodal, Multilingual, and Multitask (M3) transformers to ensure AI systems serve diverse linguistic identities with demographic fairness and structural equity.

## Publications & Achievements

* **AURA-ST: Acoustic-Unconstrained Residual Architecture for Speech Translation**
  * *Focus:* Introduces a novel residual architecture designed for speech translation that operates without rigid acoustic constraints, improving flexibility and translation robustness.
* **KIT-TIP-NLP at MultiPride: Continual Learning with Multilingual Foundation Model**
  * *Focus:* Explores continual learning strategies applied to multilingual foundation models, developed for the MultiPride shared task/benchmark.
* **RBG-AI: Benefits of Multilingual Language Models for Low-Resource Languages**
  * *Focus:* Investigates the impact and performance gains of leveraging large-scale multilingual language models specifically for underrepresented and low-resource languages.
* **RBG-AI at FadeIT: Prompted LLMs with Label Abstraction for Logical Fallacy Detection**
  * *Focus:* Demonstrates the use of prompt engineering and label abstraction techniques with Large Language Models to identify logical fallacies, submitted to the FadeIT challenge.
